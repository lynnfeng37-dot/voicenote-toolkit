# Finance semantic fields

- `performance_review`: stated absolute or relative results and their period.
- `performance_attribution`: claimed drivers of performance, separated from the result itself.
- `portfolio_change`: completed or proposed changes, with direction and magnitude when stated.
- `valuation`: methods, assumptions, multiples, price targets, and comparisons.
- `financial_metric`: reported or forecast metrics with unit, period, currency, and basis.
- `management_guidance`: guidance explicitly attributed to management.
- `investment_thesis`: the core causal case, not a generic topic summary.
- `contrarian_view`: a view explicitly presented against consensus or another speaker.
- `catalyst`: events that may change the thesis or valuation.
- `risk`: downside factors or invalidation conditions.
- `decision`: an explicit decision made during the meeting.
- `action_item`: a follow-up with owner and deadline only when those were stated.
- `open_question`: unresolved matters; do not turn them into asserted facts.

Facts, attributed opinions, and model inferences must remain distinguishable. Preserve source
segment IDs for every item.
