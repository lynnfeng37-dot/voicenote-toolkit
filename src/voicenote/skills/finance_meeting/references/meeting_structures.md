# Finance meeting structures

Choose a structure only after classifying the meeting. Omit unsupported and empty sections.

## Fund manager review

Performance and attribution; market view; portfolio positioning; sectors and assets; key
companies; risks; Q&A; summary.

## Company research

Company update; business segments; operating performance; industry and competition; management
guidance; growth drivers; valuation; catalysts; risks; Q&A.

## Internal investment meeting

Core view; supporting evidence; counterarguments; valuation; catalysts; risks; open questions;
follow-up tasks.

Other meeting types may use a content-led structure. Do not manufacture headings solely to resemble
one of these examples.
